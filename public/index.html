Research Areas

🧬 Single-cell Omics Analysis

Utilizing single-cell RNA sequencing technology to study the heterogeneity of vascular endothelial cells, analyzing cell subpopulation characteristics and functional states, and identifying disease-related cell types.

🫀 Vascular Aging Mechanisms

Exploring the molecular mechanisms of vascular endothelial cell aging, studying senescence-associated gene expression patterns, and analyzing the role of senescent cells in atherosclerosis.

🔬 Bioinformatics Methods

Developing and applying bioinformatics tools and algorithms, integrating multi-omics data, constructing disease prediction models, and discovering new therapeutic targets.
