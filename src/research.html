01

How do aging blood vessels affect the skin?

I study whether senescent endothelial cells contribute to skin aging by changing the signals they send to surrounding cells.

Period
June 2025 – present
My role
Major contributor
Status
Ongoing

The question

Endothelial cells line our blood vessels. I ask whether changes in these cells influence fibroblast behavior and the extracellular matrix that gives skin its structure.

My contribution

  • Contribute to study design and bioinformatics analysis of microvascular homeostasis in skin aging.
  • Build cell and animal models and analyze the resulting data.
  • Use single-cell analysis to investigate endothelial states and signals that may influence the surrounding skin.

02

How might organoid exosomes influence hair follicle aging?

I investigate the role of hair follicle organoid exosomes in follicle aging, including questions about the signals that support follicle cell function.

Period
September 2025 – present
My role
Major contributor
Status
Ongoing

The question

Dermal papilla cells help regulate hair growth. I ask whether signals from a vascularized model can support their function in experimental models of androgenetic alopecia.

My contribution

  • Contribute to study design for the hair follicle organoid exosome project.
  • Extract and characterize exosomes.
  • Build cell and animal models to investigate their role in hair follicle aging.
