Research system

Vascular aging, follicle repair, and AI-guided regeneration

Jackson Dai studies how senescent vascular niches reshape skin and hair repair, using single-cell biology, organoid models, and computational tools.

project 01 Vascular-skin axis in dermal aging
Skin aging biology

Deciphering the vascular-skin axis

Endothelial senescence and Novel Regulator X are framed as upstream drivers of dermal aging rather than passive consequences of tissue decline.

core question

What makes aged skin less repairable?

The project tests whether senescent endothelial programs actively reshape fibroblast behavior and extracellular matrix maintenance.

model system

Human skin and endothelial senescence

Single-cell datasets, clinical samples, endothelial culture, and fibroblast co-culture are used to map vascular niche behavior.

computational layer

Single-cell mapping to target nomination

scRNA-seq reanalysis identifies NRX-low endothelial states, inflammatory secretome programs, and candidate crosstalk signals.

translational angle

Vascular normalization and repair

Small molecules and engineered exosomes are explored as ways to restore vascular support and improve dermal regeneration.

current route

Discovery starts from public and clinical single-cell profiles, moves into mechanistic validation of NRX-deficient endothelial cells, then returns to intervention models for skin aging and wound repair.

中文摘要

本项目聚焦微血管在皮肤衰老中的主动作用，提出内皮细胞衰老及 NRX 下调可能是真皮老化的上游驱动因素。研究整合单细胞转录组、临床样本验证和分子生物学模型，绘制血管-皮肤轴的调控网络，并探索血管正常化与真皮再生的干预策略。

project 02 Angio-follicular crosstalk in alopecia
Regenerative model systems

Restoring angio-follicular crosstalk

A vascularized assembloid strategy is used to study how vascular signals may reverse senescence in alopecia-derived dermal papilla cells.

core question

Can a vascular niche rescue follicle aging?

The project asks whether vascularized hair follicle assembloids can restore missing signals in androgenetic alopecia models.

model system

vHFO assembloids and patient DPCs

Human hair follicle organoids and blood vessel organoids are fused, then tested against oxidative stress models of DPC senescence.

computational layer

Multi-omics signal discovery

RNA-seq and proteomics screen vHFO-derived exosomes for angiocrine factors and miRNAs linked to Wnt and antioxidant rescue.

translational angle

Exosome-guided follicle repair

vHFO-Exos are evaluated for their ability to restore proliferation, ALP, Versican, and hair-inductive properties in aged DPCs.

experimental route

The workflow combines assembloid construction, patient-derived DPC stress modeling, exosome profiling, and functional validation of senescence reversal.

中文摘要
