01

How do aging blood vessels affect the skin?

I study whether senescent endothelial cells contribute to skin aging by changing the signals they send to surrounding cells.

The question

Endothelial cells line our blood vessels. I ask whether changes in these cells influence fibroblast behavior and the extracellular matrix that gives skin its structure.

The approach

  • Reanalyze single-cell datasets to identify endothelial states associated with aging.
  • Study their signals in human skin samples, endothelial cultures, and fibroblast co-cultures.
  • Explore small molecules and engineered exosomes as experimental approaches to tissue repair.

02

Can vascular signals support hair follicle repair?

I use vascularized hair follicle models to investigate how signals from blood vessels may affect stressed or aging follicle cells.

The question

Dermal papilla cells help regulate hair growth. I ask whether signals from a vascularized model can support their function in experimental models of androgenetic alopecia.

The approach

  • Combine hair follicle and blood vessel organoids into vascularized assembloids.
  • Profile their exosomes using RNA sequencing and proteomics.
  • Test candidate signals in patient-derived dermal papilla cells under oxidative stress, measuring proliferation and hair-inductive properties.
