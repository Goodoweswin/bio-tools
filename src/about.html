About me

Jackson Dai · Jie Dai / 代杰

I am curious about how cells age, and how better tools can help us understand them.

I'm an M.D. student in clinical medicine (plastic surgery) at Union Hospital, Tongji Medical College, Huazhong University of Science and Technology. I expect to graduate in 2027.

My research connects skin and hair follicle aging with single-cell and spatial omics. Alongside the lab work, I founded Pilotus Studio to build software and explore AI-assisted research with a small team.

Outside the lab, I play football and founded the Tongji Football Association.
